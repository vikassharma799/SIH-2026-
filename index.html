Common questions

Short answers to what students ask first.

Where do I raise a complaint?

Pre-Matric and Post-Matric complaints go through the communication module on dbttribal.gov.in. Top Class, NFST and NOS students use the grievance module on their portal or the help-desk email shown in each scheme's details.

How does the system pick schemes?

It compares your level of study, income, age and a few facts about your course with each scheme's rules.

Is a high match a guarantee?

No. The match score is a prototype guide. The scholarship authority decides final eligibility.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Browsing and matching work without an account. Signing in only saves your history on this device.

Are the scheme details final?

No. Limits and benefits change, so confirm on scholarships.gov.in and tribal.nic.in before applying.
